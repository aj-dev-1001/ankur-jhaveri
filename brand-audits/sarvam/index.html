Brand & Marketing Audit · Sarvam (sarvam.ai)

Sarvam beats Gemini. But enterprises still don't know it.

A short audit of Sarvam's marketing footprint: where the product is ahead, and where the market's perception hasn't caught up.

By Ankur Jhaveri

At a glance

What's working

  • Models beat Gemini 3 Pro, GPT-4o on Indic benchmarks
  • 14 named enterprise deployments: SBI Life, Tata Capital, IDFC First

What's not

  • Founder LinkedIn Thought Leadership score: 31%. Co-founder: 0 posts, 30 days
  • 0 comparison pages, 0 G2 reviews, 0 gated reports

Fix: distribution and proof, not more models.

01 · Positioning & Website Copy

The homepage sells sovereign AI, but it's not what enterprises will pay for

Current H1 on landing page

"AI for all from India"

Proposed H1

"Enterprise-grade Voice, document and workflow AI solutions. Built for 22 Indian languages."

Problem

  • Language focuses on "product", not "value prop for buyer"
  • Trust badges (SOC 2, ISO 27001) sit below the fold
  • 56 of 78 homepage images have no alt text (impacts SEO)

Fix

  • Move certifications into the hero band
  • Add a logo strip: SBI Life, Tata Capital, IDFC First, HealthPlix
  • Fix the 56 missing alt tags
ImpactImmediate brand clarity. An enterprise BFSI buyer knows exactly what it will get with Sarvam.

02 · The Benchmark Gap

The claim is everywhere. Independent proof is nowhere.

Problem

BEATS GEMINI 3 PRO
Business Standard, Business Today
BEATS DEEPSEEK OCR
Covered by Tom's Guide (int'l press)
20,000+ VOTES
Sarvam's own study, not 3rd-party

Every article cites Sarvam's own release. None link to a neutral leaderboard.

Fix

  • List results on a neutral leaderboard: Hugging Face Open ASR or Artificial Analysis so the claim isn't self-graded.
  • Publish full methodology + raw eval logs alongside every benchmark claim. This can then be picked up by other publications.
  • Link comparison pages to the neutral listing once live (not just to Sarvam's own blog).
ImpactCategory leadership, independently provable. Higher chances of it getting picked organically by publications and media outlets.
