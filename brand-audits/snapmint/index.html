B2B Marketing Audit · Snapmint (snapmintbusiness.com)

Snapmint is signing big brands, but the marketing needs to catch up.

A short audit of Snapmint's B2B marketing — where the assets exist, where they're not reaching the buyer, and what I'd do in the first 90 days.

By Ankur Jhaveri · Prepared alongside my application for Senior Manager, B2B Marketing

What's working

Marquee logos landing

  • Zepto, boAt, WHOOP going live in 2026
  • Plus BIBA, Liberty Shoes and more

Solid marketing foundations

  • B2B site: full schema stack, active blog, biweekly cadence
  • CCO Amitt Sharma on the speaking circuit — Inc42 D2C Summit, Aug 2026

The business is doing its job. The rest of this covers where the marketing can catch up.

The case studies don't talk to Snapmint's ideal client.

Larger clients need bigger case studies.

04 · Unused assets

Two things Snapmint already has, but neither is turned on.

Founder LinkedIn

3

Authored posts in 8 months.

The Series B "saying no" post: 350 likes, 67 comments. He can write. A regular cadence is missing.

Comparable: Cred / Razorpay founders post 2–4× a week.

YouTube case studies

12,576

Views on the Superkicks case study video (04-08-2026).

Embed count on the Superkicks case study page: zero. Same across every other case study.

FixGet a ghostwriter to post case studies about the industry, from the founder and leadership's profiles.

05 · What I'd do first

30 / 60 / 90.

Days 0–30

Surface what exists

No new content, no new hires.

  • Add 12 missing case studies to sitemap
  • Embed YT videos on their case study pages
  • Move #hiring posts to a careers handle
  • Add an enterprise-tier landing route
Days 30–60

Turn on distribution

Assets → audiences.

  • Founder LinkedIn ghostwriter, 2 posts/week
  • Company page: 3 brand/customer posts/week (recruiting removed)
  • Ship a monthly D2C conversion benchmark (gated) — first owned lead magnet
  • 5 marquee customers seed Shopify App Store reviews
Days 60–90

Build the enterprise credibility loop

Compounding starts.

  • Zepto / boAt / BIBA case studies published
  • Newsletter cadence steady, first 500 subs
  • ABM live against 20 named enterprise accounts

Snapmint's assets are already in the building. The first job is to make them reach the buyer.
