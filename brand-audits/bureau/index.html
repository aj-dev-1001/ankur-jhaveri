Brand & Marketing Audit · Bureau (bureau.id)

Bureau does the work.
Buyers can't find it.

A short audit of Bureau's marketing footprint — where the brand is under-delivering against its assets, and what needs work.

By Ankur Jhaveri

At a glance

What's working

  • Category-leading reports
  • Physical events with senior BFSI audience

What's not

  • Homepage, LinkedIn, G2, Google: all are silent
  • 17 reports, 6 forums, 2 awards, but none of them are reaching the buyer

Fix: distribution and surfacing, not more content.

01 · Positioning & Website Copy

The positioning names a category, not a customer

Current H1

"AI-Powered Unified Risk Decisioning for the Entire Customer Life Cycle"

Proposed H1

"Stop fraud in real time. Trusted by 200+ fintechs across India, MENA and SEA."

Problem

  • Category jargon, no specifics
  • No persona, geography, or proof above the fold
  • "200+ global brands" sits only in meta description
  • No trust badges (ISO 27001, SOC 2)

Fix

  • Rewrite H1: persona + outcome + proof
  • Trust strip: ISO 27001, ET BFSI Award ×2, SAMA-ready
  • Move 3 flagship reports into hero block
  • A/B test new layout in 4 weeks
ImpactA clear message makes demand gen stronger for users visiting the website.

  • 17 research reports: Great thought leadership content, but buried on website.
  • ET BFSI Finnext Award: Invisible on website.
  • No weekly newsletter: 186 blogs + 17 reports are feeding no owned audience.

Fix

  • Launch new IP (Bureau Fraud Briefs): A weekly publication that goes out to the target ICP; builds high recall and thought leadership.
  • Add major logos on website for social proof.
  • Mentions in mainstream publications: Work with a PR agency to add 2-4 free media mentions/month (easily achievable).
  • Instate a MENA fraud report (Saudi Arabia + UAE): Completes geography set, doubles as newsletter.
ImpactBrand building + lead gen. No competitor in India / SEA identity space runs a regular publication. Bureau owns this.

No rebrand, no redesign. Bureau's assets exist; the first job is to make them reach the buyer.
